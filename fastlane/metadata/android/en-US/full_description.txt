Plan swimming sessions, record duration, effort and notes, and follow your progress. You can also record body weight and extra activities such as stretching and meditation.

Records are saved on your device; external copies are optional. The app has no login, ads, trackers or internet permission.

Settings let you save and restore backups, protect files with a password, and select an email app to send a backup to yourself. Sharing is manual and requires confirmation in the selected app.

Includes a local timer and integration with the Android system timer. This diary does not provide medical or nutritional advice. Source code is licensed under Apache-2.0.

The app interface is currently available in Brazilian Portuguese.

Optional encrypted automatic backups to a file you select in Google Drive or another Android document provider. Each user authorizes their destination; the provider handles cloud uploads. Manual password-based restore; no automatic merging between phones.
